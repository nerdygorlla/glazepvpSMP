Features

A complete economy loop

Farm drops, sell them, spend the money and turn your profit into bigger farms.

/sell

Live economy

Sell crops, mob drops and ores for money. Check prices with /worth and your totals with /sellhistory.

/ah

Auction house

List loot for other players, search by category and flip items for profit.

/order

Orders board

Post a buy order for what you need and let other players fill it for you.

/shards

Shards

A second currency earned in the AFK zone and from PvP. Spend it in the shard shop and on crates.

/rtp

Random teleport

Drop into a random spot in the wild and start a base wherever you land.

/team

Teams

Invite friends, set team homes, use team chat and manage PvP between members.

spawners

Spawner farms

Stack spawners and build money farms. The drops are your main income.

/bounty

Bounties and duels

Put a price on a rival, or challenge someone to a duel in the ranked arena.

Rules

Play fair

PvP and raiding are part of the game. Cheating is not.

  • No hacked clients, x-ray, or macros that give an unfair advantage.
  • No exploiting bugs or duping items. Report them to staff instead.
  • No scamming, and no selling accounts, items or currency for real money.
  • Keep chat respectful: no hate speech, harassment or spam.
  • One account per player in the AFK zone. Bots there are banned.
